PARTY WALL NOTICE

From: Mrs Adaeze Okafor, 16 Orchard Lane, Fenbridge ("the Building Owner")
To: Mr Robert Hartley and Mrs Susan Hartley, 14 Orchard Lane, Fenbridge ("the Adjoining Owners")

Date of notice: 1 September 2026

1. The Building Owner intends to carry out work to the party wall separating 14 and 16 Orchard Lane, namely the wall forming the side of the single-storey rear outbuildings of both properties.

2. The proposed work is:
   (a) to cut into the party wall to insert steel beams supporting a new single-storey rear extension at number 16;
   (b) to raise the height of the party wall by approximately 600 millimetres along a length of 4.2 metres.

3. The Building Owner intends to begin the work on 1 October 2026.

4. A drawing of the proposed work is NOT enclosed with this notice and will follow under separate cover.

5. The Building Owner proposes Mr Leon Harper of Harper Surveying as agreed surveyor.

6. If you do not reply to this notice within fourteen days, you will be treated as having dissented.

Signed: Adaeze Okafor
