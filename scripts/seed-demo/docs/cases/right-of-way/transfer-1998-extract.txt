EXTRACT FROM TRANSFER DATED 14 OCTOBER 1998
(Copy supplied by the client. Extract only. Clauses not relevant to access omitted.)

Seller: Ashcombe Estates Limited
Buyer: Brookfield Farm Limited

Property transferred: the land known as Brookfield Farm, Lower Ashcombe, shown edged red on the plan attached ("the Property").

Retained land: the land of the Seller known as Ashcombe Home Farm, shown edged blue on the plan attached ("the Retained Land").

CLAUSE 9: RIGHTS GRANTED

9.1 The Seller grants to the Buyer and its successors in title, for the benefit of the Property and each and every part of it, a right of way at all times and for all purposes connected with the use of the Property as agricultural land, on foot and with or without vehicles, animals and machinery, over the track coloured brown on the plan ("the Track").

9.2 The Track runs from the public highway known as Ashcombe Road, through the Retained Land, to the northern gate of the Property, a distance of approximately 640 metres.

9.3 The Buyer shall contribute one half of the reasonable cost of maintaining the Track in a condition suitable for agricultural vehicles.

CLAUSE 10: GATES

10.1 The Seller may maintain gates across the Track at the points marked G1 and G2 on the plan, provided that the gates are kept unlocked or that the Buyer is provided with the means of opening them at all times.

10.2 The Seller shall not place or permit anything on the Track that prevents its use by agricultural vehicles of the kind in use on the Property.

PLAN NOTES

The Track coloured brown is shown on the plan as having a width of 3.5 metres throughout.
Gate G1 stands at the junction of the Track with Ashcombe Road.
Gate G2 stands 410 metres along the Track from Ashcombe Road, at the boundary between two fields of the Retained Land.
