WITNESS STATEMENT
Matter: Brookfield Farm – right of way, locked gate (MAT-2026-009)
Statement of: Mr Thomas Reed, farm manager, Brookfield Farm Limited
Taken by: Daniel Croft, Wakefield & Croft LLP
Date: 3 September 2026

1. I have been farm manager at Brookfield Farm since 2004. I am responsible for the day-to-day running of the farm, including moving machinery, livestock and produce between the farm and Ashcombe Road.

2. For as long as I have worked at the farm, our main route to the road has been the track that runs through Ashcombe Home Farm. We use it every day. In the harvest months we use it many times a day, with tractors, trailers and a combine harvester. Our grain lorries collect from the farm using the same track.

3. Until March 2026 there were two field gates across the track, one at the road and one about halfway along. Both were ordinary five-bar gates held shut with a chain and hook. They were never locked.

4. On 4 March 2026 Ashcombe Estates replaced the halfway gate with a steel gate fitted with an electronic keypad. Since then the gate has been kept shut. We have not been given the code. Each time we need to pass, one of us has to telephone the Ashcombe estate office and wait for someone to come and open it. On weekdays this usually takes between 20 and 40 minutes. At weekends there is often no one to answer at all.

5. On 9 June 2026 Ashcombe Estates placed four large stone planters along the side of the track, about 30 metres before the keypad gate. I measured the gap between the planters and the opposite verge at 2.4 metres. Our combine harvester is 3.1 metres wide and can no longer get through. During the August harvest we had to route the combine through a neighbour's fields, by arrangement, at a cost to the farm.

6. I wrote to the Ashcombe estate office on 12 March 2026 and again on 15 June 2026, asking for the keypad code and for the planters to be moved. I have given copies of both letters to Daniel Croft. I have had no reply to either.

7. Delivery drivers and our vet have also been held up at the gate. On 21 July 2026 a vet attending a calving had to leave her vehicle at the gate and walk the remaining distance, about 230 metres.

8. I have photographs of the old gate, the new keypad gate and the planters, all dated.

I believe the facts stated in this witness statement are true.

Thomas Reed
