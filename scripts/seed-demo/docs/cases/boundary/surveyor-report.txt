BOUNDARY SURVEY REPORT
12 and 14 Orchard Lane, Fenbridge
Prepared by: Marsh & Holt Chartered Surveyors
Instructed by: Wakefield & Croft LLP, on behalf of Mr and Mrs Hartley
Matter reference: MAT-2026-014
Date of inspection: 2 September 2026
Date of report: 9 September 2026

1. SCOPE

1.1 We were asked to establish the line of the boundary between 12 and 14 Orchard Lane at the rear of the two gardens, and to compare it with the position of the close-boarded fence erected by the owner of number 12 in June 2026.

1.2 This report is prepared for the purpose of advice and possible negotiation. If the matter proceeds, we understand the parties may agree to instruct a single joint expert.

2. DOCUMENTS EXAMINED

2.1 The 1974 conveyance that first divided the land into the two plots, which includes a plan drawn at a scale of 1:500 with measurements marked.

2.2 The current title plans for both properties, at a scale of 1:1250.

2.3 Photographs supplied by Mr Hartley taken in 2009 and 2018.

3. FINDINGS ON SITE

3.1 Along the front two-thirds of the gardens the boundary is marked by a beech hedge, which has been in place since at least 1974 according to the conveyance plan. There is no ditch.

3.2 At the rear, five original concrete fence posts remain in the ground on the number 14 side of the new fence. Their positions match the line on the 1974 conveyance plan to within 5 centimetres.

3.3 The June 2026 fence departs from the line of the original posts at a point 11.2 metres from the rear wall of number 14. From there it runs at an angle into the garden of number 14. At the rear corner of the gardens it stands 41 centimetres from the line of the original posts.

3.4 From Mr Hartley's 2018 photographs, the 2011 fence stood approximately 28 centimetres from the original line at the rear corner. The June 2026 fence is therefore a further 13 centimetres or so into number 14.

3.5 The disputed strip is a triangle running 11.2 metres along the boundary, widening from nothing to 41 centimetres. Its area is approximately 2.3 square metres.

4. CONCLUSIONS

4.1 In our opinion the boundary at the rear of the gardens runs along the line of the original concrete posts, which follows the 1974 conveyance plan.

4.2 Both the 2011 fence and the June 2026 fence stand on land within the title of number 14 at the rear of the gardens.

4.3 The current title plans cannot resolve the question. At 1:1250, the width of the plotted line alone represents a strip on the ground wider than the disputed area.
