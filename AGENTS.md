The deeper version of this problem

A new tab solves losing the answer right now, but the answer still disappears on refresh, and a colleague can't be sent a link to it. That's what query history fixes: save each question, its answer and the ticked documents to a queries table, so the case page lists past research and each entry has its own URL.
